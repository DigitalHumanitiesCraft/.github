# Digital Humanities Craft

We develop research software, research data workflows and digital editions,
and we practise agentic engineering: tools, workflows and knowledge bases
built with coding agents. We also offer training and consulting on
generative AI. Our clients are universities, cultural institutions, companies
and public administration. We are based in Kainbach near Graz, Austria.

## Where to start

- [dhcraft.org](https://dhcraft.org) presents our services, selected projects and the team.
- [Promptotyping](https://dhcraft.org/Promptotyping/) is our working practice for turning
  research data and their contexts into research artefacts.
- [fancy (research) tools & workflows](https://dhcraft.org/en/fancy-research-tools/)
  describes how we build tools, workflows and knowledge bases with coding agents
  for and with clients.
- The [blog](https://dhcraft.org/excellence/blog/) publishes our longer texts under CC BY 4.0.
- Our [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@DigitalHumanitiesCraft) carries
  talks and webinars on knowledge, context and agentic engineering.
